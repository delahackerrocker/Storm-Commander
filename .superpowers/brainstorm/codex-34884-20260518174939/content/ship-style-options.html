Choose A Ship Set Direction

Each option keeps the required 12 PNG filenames, but the visual language changes how strongly the ships read as chess pieces versus a fleet.

A. Readable Fleet Icons

Bold cartoon silhouettes, thick outline, simple cockpit colors, and rank-specific profiles. Best for board readability.

B. Character Squadrons

More playful angles and exaggerated personalities. Livelier, but slightly harder to read at tiny sizes.

C. Emblem Fleet

Ships share similar hulls and use crown, tower, lance, wing, and shield markings to identify piece roles.
